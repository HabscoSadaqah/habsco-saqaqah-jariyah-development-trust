Savings rules

How qualification works
• The six-month clock starts from your qualifying Savings start date.
• Any completed Savings debit resets the six-month qualification period from that debit.
• After six consecutive months, your maximum application amount is twice your current Savings balance.
• Full withdrawal moves the entire Savings balance to your wallet; partial withdrawals are not allowed.
• Savings payments, plan changes and withdrawals require Transaction PIN confirmation.
