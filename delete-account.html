Delete Your HABSCO Account

This page is the external account-deletion resource for the HABSCO Android application and HABSCO online service.

Request deletion

To request deletion, send an email from the email address associated with your HABSCO account. Include your full name and HABSCO member/account identifier if available. Do not send your password or transaction PIN.

Request Account Deletion

What happens next

We will verify the request and take appropriate steps to delete the HABSCO account and associated personal data that we are permitted to delete.

Data that may be retained

Certain transaction, accounting, security, fraud-prevention or regulatory records may need to be retained for a lawful period. Where retention is required, the data will be retained only for that purpose and handled according to our privacy policy.
