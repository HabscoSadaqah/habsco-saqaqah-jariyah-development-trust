Share Rules

• Minimum: ₦100,000 per purchase.
• Multiple purchases: Buy shares as often as you want, subject to available wallet funds.
• Maturity: Each purchase matures after 1 year.
• Lock: Once activated, only an authorized admin can cancel/terminate it.
• Early termination: 5% charge on principal before maturity.
• Dividend: Approved dividend at maturity goes to Dividend Account and can be withdrawn to Available to Spend.
• Security: Purchase and dividend withdrawal require Transaction PIN.
