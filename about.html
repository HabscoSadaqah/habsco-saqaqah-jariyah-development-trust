WHO WE ARE

Building a Legacy of Continuous Charity

Habsco Sadaqah Jariyah Development Trust is a non-profit organization committed to transforming lives through sustainable charitable projects.

We believe that small acts of kindness today can create a lasting impact for tomorrow. Our work focuses on education, clean water, mosque development, tree planting and community development.

Our Vision

Create lasting positive change through sustainable charitable projects benefiting generations.

Our Mission

Improve lives by supporting mosques, schools, clean water projects, tree planting initiatives and community-based programs.

Our Values

Compassion, integrity, transparency, sustainability and community.

Our Promise

To serve responsibly and make every contribution count.
