## Required Play Console work
1. Create/verify a Google Play Organization developer account.
2. Complete Data safety accurately.
3. Complete the Financial features declaration.
4. Provide any required financial/lending documentation.
5. Create the store listing, screenshots, icon and feature graphic.
6. Upload a signed Android App Bundle.
7. Use internal/closed testing before production.

## Important
HABSCO includes wallet, transfer, cooperative and/or loan-related financial functionality. Do not submit financial declarations or licensing information as completed until the actual services, licenses and operating model have been verified.

## Account deletion
The app and website must provide a discoverable deletion request path. Deletion must actually remove associated personal data except information that must lawfully be retained.

## Release
The production release must be signed with a secure upload key. Never commit keystores, passwords, signing keys or service-account credentials to GitHub.
