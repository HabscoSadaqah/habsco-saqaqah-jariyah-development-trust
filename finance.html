About the Cooperative

Building financial support through trust and cooperation

The Habsco Free-Interest Multipurpose Cooperative Society is designed to bring members together in a transparent and supportive community. Members can participate in approved cooperative activities, maintain their account securely and access services available through the society.

The programme promotes responsible financial support without interest. Where applicable, Qard Hasan (Interest-Free Benevolent Loan) provides a compassionate loan pathway for eligible members, subject to verification, programme policy and available funds.

Our goal is to make community finance easier to understand, easier to access and guided by trust, accountability and dignity.
