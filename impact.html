FROM RELIEF TO SELF-RELIANCE

Creating Lasting Impact

We focus on practical programmes that relieve hardship today while creating opportunities, sustainable resources and lasting benefit for communities and future generations.

01

💧 Solar-Powered Clean Water

Solar-powered boreholes and sustainable access to clean water for communities, schools and vulnerable households.

02

🏪 Waqf & Sustainable Income

Waqf shops and other income-generating assets designed to create continuing resources for charitable projects.

03

🤝 Qard Hasan

Interest-free benevolent assistance for genuine needs, livelihood activities and small businesses, with repayments helping the fund reach more beneficiaries.

04

🛠️ Vocational & Digital Skills

Free practical and digital skills training that helps young people and women gain employment or build sustainable businesses.

05

👩‍💼 Women & Youth Empowerment

Entrepreneurship support, mentorship, tools and livelihood opportunities that promote dignity, independence and self-reliance.

06

🎓 Education & Scholarships

Educational support, learning materials, scholarships and opportunities for disadvantaged children and students.

07

🏥 Healthcare & Medical Support

Medical assistance and community health support for vulnerable people who need help accessing essential care.

08

🍚 Food & Emergency Relief

Food assistance and urgent support for vulnerable families and communities during hardship and emergencies.

09

🏘️ Community Development

Community-led projects that improve living conditions, strengthen resilience and create opportunities for long-term development.

SUSTAINABILITY

Impact That Continues

Our goal is not only to respond to immediate needs, but to build systems that continue serving people. Waqf assets can generate sustainable income, while Qard Hasan can recycle repaid principal to support additional beneficiaries.

We will continue to publish verified project information, beneficiaries, locations, photographs and impact updates as programmes grow.

Building a Legacy of Continuous Charity through sustainable projects that benefit communities and generations.
