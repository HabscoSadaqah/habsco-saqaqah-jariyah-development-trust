WHAT WE DO

Programmes Built Around Lasting Benefit

Habsco Sadaqah Jariyah Development Trust focuses on practical initiatives that strengthen communities, restore dignity and create sustainable sources of benefit.

Our approach connects relief, opportunity and sustainability — so support is not only helpful today, but can contribute to a stronger future.

SUSTAINABILITY

Impact That Keeps Giving

Our goal is not only to respond to immediate needs, but to build systems that continue serving people. Waqf assets can generate sustainable income, while Qard Hasan can recycle repaid principal to support additional beneficiaries.

As programmes grow, we aim to publish verified project information, beneficiaries, locations, photographs and impact updates.
