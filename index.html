ABOUT US

Building Continuous Benefit

Habsco Sadaqah Jariyah Development Trust is committed to sustainable charity, community welfare and projects that create lasting benefit.

Our work includes Sadaqah Jariyah, Waqf, clean water, education, skills development and Qard Hasan — benevolent, interest-free financing designed to help eligible members meet important needs and build sustainable livelihoods.
